# Data privacy and responsible-use statement

## Educational status

AI Rx Live Demo Studio is workshop software. It is not a medical device, diagnostic system, prescribing system, electronic health record, or substitute for professional judgement. Local examples are fictional or abstract simulations.

## Prohibited workshop input

Do not enter identifiable patient information, patient audio, photographs, medical images, reports, prescriptions, contact details, identifiers, confidential institutional documents, or data that participants are not authorised to share. Removing a name may not be enough to prevent re-identification.

## Human responsibility

A qualified healthcare professional must compare clinically relevant output with the source, verify evidence, correct errors, retain uncertainty, and approve the final use. Do not automate diagnosis, prescribing, triage disposition, or patient communication without an approved clinical workflow and direct professional review.

## External services

Official links open third-party products. Each provider controls its own account terms, data practices, retention, security, subprocessors, pricing, geography, and product status. A link in this project is not an endorsement or confirmation that the service is appropriate for protected health information.

Before institutional use, review the exact product and version, intended use, contract, data flow, storage location, retention, deletion, access control, audit logs, model improvement practices, incident response, human override, evidence, regulation, and local law. Record the decision and its owner.

## Medical-image simulations

Files under `assets/sample_medical_images/` are project-generated PNG placeholders. They contain no patient data and no diagnostic ground truth. Illustrative confidence values are invented solely to teach interface scepticism. They must not be shown as examples of model performance.

## Research and copyright

Upload only papers and documents that are openly shareable or otherwise authorised. Check every generated citation and claim against the original source. Source-grounded output can still be incomplete or misinterpreted.

## Professional communication

Do not use a patient story, image, report, or testimonial without appropriate consent and institutional or legal clearance. Review drafts for unsupported claims, guaranteed outcomes, fear-based language, promotional exaggeration, missing evidence, conflicts of interest, and the boundary between education and individual medical advice.

## Reporting a concern

Stop the demonstration if sensitive information is entered or a clinically unsafe output appears. Do not copy or distribute the material. Follow the institution’s privacy, information-security, clinical-governance, and incident-reporting procedures.
